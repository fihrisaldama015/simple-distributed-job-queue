Simple Distributed Job Queue Simulation

This simulates a background job queue: create jobs, watch failures retry with backoff, and see how a fixed pool of workers handles a burst of work.

i Runs a job that fails on its first two attempts and succeeds on the third, so you can watch the retry policy back off and recover. i Enqueues 50 jobs at once - one in five is the unstable job, so some will bounce back to Pending mid-drain to retry. Only 8 workers run at a time, so you'll see the queue's concurrency limit and its retry policy working under load, together.
